🥗 Diet plans

    Cheaper swaps that work just as well
    • Rui or katla too costly this week? Pangas, tilapia or small fish give the same protein for less.
    • Almonds → a handful of roasted chola (buut bhaja) or a few peanuts. Cheaper and just as filling.
    • Apple → guava, amra, jambura or papaya. Local fruit costs less and has more fibre.
    • Chicken price up? 2 eggs, soybean chunks or thick dal cover the protein.
    • Sweet doi → tok doi made at home from milk. Cheaper and no added sugar.
    • Brown bread from the shop → atta ruti at home. Cheaper, and one ruti is only about 80 kcal.
    • Oil: measure 1 tablespoon per person when cooking the family curry. The whole family benefits.
    • Buy shak and sobji in season from the bazaar: lau, pepe, kumra, dherosh and shak are cheap all year.
    All 20 plans are about 1200 to 1400 kcal, diabetes-friendly (half the plate vegetables, a palm of protein, measured bhat or ruti), and use food a family already cooks. Your doctor's or dietitian's advice comes first.
